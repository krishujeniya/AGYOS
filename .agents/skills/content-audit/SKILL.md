description: >-
  Audit an existing social media account and its content — the retrospective reality-check of what's working and what isn't. Use when someone wants to "audit my content/account,"
  "what's working and what isn't," "clean up my profile/feed," "I post a lot but it's not working," or to
  triage an existing account before replanning. Produces a keep/kill/refresh triage, maps pillar coverage,
  catches voice drift, and fixes profile fundamentals — then a quick-wins-vs-long-term roadmap. Uses the
  AUDIT framework. Reads brand-profile + content-pillars + social-strategy (the plan) and
  analytics-and-reporting (the data) first. It reads WoopSocial post analytics on covered platforms and native dashboards otherwise, scores on
  signal not vanity, recommends rather than auto-deletes, and never fabricates. Feeds content-recycling,
  content-pillars, profile-optimization, voice-builder, and social-strategy. Distinct from
  analytics-and-reporting (ongoing measurement) and competitor-analysis (rivals).

  - "## The POV: activity isn't progress\nPlenty of accounts look busy — daily reels, carousels, likes trickling in, followers inching up — while\n**nothing actually moves.** A content audit finds the **signal under the busy-ness**: what gets *saved*\nand *shared* (not just liked), which pillars are starved or bloated, where the voice has drifted (or\nwhere your winners say it should **evolve**), and which profile fundamentals quietly leak followers.\nStrategy is forward-looking; the audit is the **retrospective reality-check** that informs it.\n\n"

  - "## The framework: AUDIT\n(Depth: `references/the-audit-framework.md`.)\n- **A — Assemble the inventory:** ~90 days of output by platform/format/pillar/time + native metrics; pull\n  the plan you're auditing against.\n- **U — Uncover the signal:** score on **saves/shares/replies + the goal-mapped KPI** (not vanity);\n  surface top/bottom performers + patterns by format/time/pillar.\n- **D — Diagnose pillars, voice & profile:** pillar whitespace (→ content-pillars); voice drift **both\n  ways** (→ voice-builder); profile fundamentals + cadence (→ profile-optimization).\n- **I — Iterate: keep / kill / refresh:** scale winners (→ content-recycling), cut chronic/off-brand/\n  non-native, refresh good-but-dated. **Refresh before kill; human approves kills.**\n- **T — Turn into a roadmap:** strengths/weaknesses/opportunities; **quick wins vs long-term**; owners +\n  timelines; hand off. **No spreadsheet graveyard.**\n\n"
