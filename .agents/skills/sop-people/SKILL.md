# SOP: People Operations (Hiring & Talent)

This standard operating procedure governs talent screening, interview scorecard creation, and hiring evaluations.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Intake & Role Profile Definition**:
   - Ingest job description, core technical competencies, and evaluation rubric.
   - Set task risk tier to R0 (screening/evaluation) or R1 (scorecard draft).
   - Automated candidate rejections or formal offers are strictly forbidden.

2. **Objective Candidate Assessment**:
   - Evaluate resume against explicit competency benchmarks.
   - Score candidates based solely on verified technical qualifications and portfolio evidence.

3. **Interview Packet Preparation**:
   - Formulate structured, behavioral, and technical questions designed to test competency.
   - Supply rubric scorecards for human interviewers.

4. **Human Handoff**:
   - Present synthesized evaluation to human hiring authority for final decisions.
