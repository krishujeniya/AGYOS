  - "# SOP: People Operations (Hiring & Talent)\n\nThis standard operating procedure governs talent screening, interview scorecard creation, and hiring evaluations.\n\n"
  - "## Procedure Steps\n\n1. **Intake & Role Profile Definition**:\n   - Ingest job description, core technical competencies, and evaluation rubric.\n   - Set task risk tier to R0 (screening/evaluation) or R1 (scorecard draft).\n   - Automated candidate rejections or formal offers are strictly forbidden.\n\n2. **Objective Candidate Assessment**:\n   - Evaluate resume against explicit competency benchmarks.\n   - Score candidates based solely on verified technical qualifications and portfolio evidence.\n\n3. **Interview Packet Preparation**:\n   - Formulate structured, behavioral, and technical questions designed to test competency.\n   - Supply rubric scorecards for human interviewers.\n\n4. **Human Handoff**:\n   - Present synthesized evaluation to human hiring authority for final decisions.\n"
