## Agent Guidelines & Workflows

### 1. Financial Infrastructure & Invoicing
When asked to set up or audit a freelancer's financial workflow:
- Reference [06A-FINANCE-TRACKING.md](references/06A-FINANCE-TRACKING.md) to split incoming revenue: 30% for taxes, 20% for business development, 50% for owner salary/expenses.
- Guide the freelancer to set up Wise or Payoneer for international client transactions to minimize conversion fees.
- Design clean invoices containing all legally required fields.

### 2. Legal Contracts & Clauses
When drafting or reviewing client contracts:
- Reference [06B-LEGAL-CONTRACTS.md](references/06B-LEGAL-CONTRACTS.md) to verify that the contract includes the **12 Essential Clauses** (IP Assignment upon final payment, late payment penalties, clear termination terms, and governing jurisdiction).
- Advise on NDA boundaries to protect the freelancer's background IP (boilerplates, reusable utilities).

### 3. CRM & Operational Automation
When designing an automated admin workflow:
- Reference [06C-AUTOMATION-STACK.md](references/06C-AUTOMATION-STACK.md) to choose the correct automation framework (Zapier, Make, or self-hosted n8n).
- Set up the **CRM Lead Capture Engine**: Typeform/Cal.com $\rightarrow$ n8n/Zapier $\rightarrow$ Slack $\rightarrow$ Airtable.

### 4. Time Defense & Calendars
When auditing calendar schedules or time-management practices:
- Reference [06D-TIME-MANAGEMENT.md](references/06D-TIME-MANAGEMENT.md) to structure a calendar using **Deep Work Architecture** (blocking 3-4 hour uninterrupted periods for coding, separating client calls to specific afternoons).
- Formulate async-first email or Slack boundaries to block constant client interruptions.

### 5. Scaling Beyond Freelancing
When asked to scale operations, raise prices, or hire team members:
- Reference [06E-SCALING-SYSTEMS.md](references/06E-SCALING-SYSTEMS.md) to productize complex services (e.g., "ML model audit for $2,500" with a fixed scope, clear inputs, and standardized outputs).
- Structure sub-contracting agreements to delegate basic tasks while preserving quality control.
