  - "## Agent Guidelines & Workflows\n\n"
  - "### 1. Outbound Prospecting & Lead Gen\nWhen asked to build lists or identify potential leads:\n- Reference [02A-PROSPECTING-SYSTEM.md](references/02A-PROSPECTING-SYSTEM.md) to set up filters on Apollo.io and LinkedIn Sales Navigator.\n- Implement **Signal-Based Prospecting**: focus on target companies that recently raised funding, hired new executives, or published job postings in target technology stacks.\n- Guide the freelancer to monitor Reddit and communities for direct problem signals.\n\n"
  - "### 2. Cold Outreach Execution\nWhen drafting cold emails, DMs, or follow-up sequences:\n- Reference [02B-COLD-OUTREACH.md](references/02B-COLD-OUTREACH.md) to apply the **Ultra-Short Value Offer** format (40–60 words, personalized hook, specific outcome proof, low-friction call-to-action).\n- Structure follow-up campaigns using the 5-email sequence: Day 1 (Value Offer) $\\rightarrow$ Day 3 (Case Study snippet) $\\rightarrow$ Day 7 (Resource/Quick win) $\\rightarrow$ Day 10 (Direct question) $\\rightarrow$ Day 14 (Breakup email).\n\n"
  - "### 3. Freelancing Platform Optimization\nWhen asked to optimize freelancer profiles or proposal strategies on platforms:\n- Reference [02C-PLATFORM-STRATEGY.md](references/02C-PLATFORM-STRATEGY.md) to structure profiles for SEO and JSS (Job Success Score) maintenance on Upwork.\n- Compare Upwork's commission model with Contra's flat-fee model ($29/contract) to guide pricing and platform selection.\n\n"
  - "### 4. Content Marketing & Organic Traffic\nWhen writing LinkedIn posts or building content plans:\n- Reference [02D-CONTENT-MARKETING.md](references/02D-CONTENT-MARKETING.md) to write hooks and body copy optimized for **LinkedIn Dwell Time** (dwell time $>$ simple likes).\n- Leverage the content repurposing flywheel to break down one long-form asset (case study, technical article) into 10 high-impact social posts.\n\n"
  - "### 5. Referral loops\nWhen establishing customer retention and referral systems:\n- Reference [02E-REFERRAL-ENGINE.md](references/02E-REFERRAL-ENGINE.md) to execute the **3-Ask Testimonial Method** (ask at kickoff, ask at delivery, ask 30 days post-delivery).\n- Establish double-sided referral incentives and strategic agency partnerships.\n\n---\n\n"
