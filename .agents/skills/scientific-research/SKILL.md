# Scientific research

Use this runbook to structure a research task. The linked source is a changing catalog of domain-specific skills and databases, not evidence that every workflow is installed in this checkout.

## Workflow

1. Define the research question, relevant population or system, outcomes, date range, and inclusion limits.
2. Choose primary papers, datasets, or authoritative domain sources that fit the question. Record the search terms and access date.
3. For each source, note its design, sample or data, methods, controls, outcome, limitations, and conflicts of interest when reported.
4. Separate measured findings from hypotheses and interpretation. Keep citations attached to the claims they support.
5. Compare evidence without hiding disagreement or uncertainty. Do not infer causation from association or generalize beyond the sampled population.
6. Check current domain sources for time-sensitive claims. Label unsupported or unverified claims rather than filling gaps with guesses.

For specialized methods and source-specific procedures, inspect the current [Scientific Agent Skills catalog](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills) and select the relevant domain skill. Do not treat this general workflow as medical, legal, or other professional advice.

## Limitations
