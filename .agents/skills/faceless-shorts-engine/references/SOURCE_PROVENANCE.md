# Source provenance and boundaries

The faceless-shorts workflow is based on the user-supplied faceless-shorts package, especially
its README, skill instructions, six styles/*/STYLE.md files, and setup, voice, word-timing, and
capture scripts.

The separately supplied nafay3d_style_prompts_recreated.md says its seven style prompts and
screenshot-cloning prompt were newly written from public descriptions and are not the email-gated
originals. It also says the exact source prompts were unavailable in that extraction. Do not
describe those eight prompts as original, official, or verified. The separate Untitled document.txt
has no provenance statement in the provided file; treat it as an unverified prompt/reference dump.

Those motion-graphics prompts are not the six faceless-short styles. Keep the toolkits, style names,
model details, and implementation steps separate. When relying on either prompt document, label
what it says and what has been independently checked.
