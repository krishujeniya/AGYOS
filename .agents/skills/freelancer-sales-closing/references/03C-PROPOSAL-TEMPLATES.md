## 1. Proposal Structure

### The 10 Essential Components [^41^][^18^]

| # | Component | Purpose | Length |
|---|-----------|---------|--------|
| 1 | **Header** | Client name, project title, date, your contact | 3-4 lines |
| 2 | **Project Summary** | What, why, and outcome in 2-3 sentences | 50-75 words |
| 3 | **Goals & Success Criteria** | Measurable outcomes (not tasks) | 3-5 bullets |
| 4 | **Scope of Work** | What's included + explicitly excluded | Detailed |
| 5 | **Deliverables** | Tangible outputs with format/quantity | List |
| 6 | **Timeline & Milestones** | Week-by-week with dependencies | Table |
| 7 | **Pricing & Payment** | Fixed price, schedule, methods | Clear |
| 8 | **Terms & Assumptions** | IP, cancellation, communication norms | 5-7 bullets |
| 9 | **Revisions & Changes** | What's included, how extras are billed | 2-3 bullets |
| 10 | **Acceptance & Next Steps** | How to say yes, what happens next | CTA |

### The 2-Minute Skim Test

A busy client should understand your proposal in 2 minutes:
- **Page 1:** Problem → Solution → Price → CTA
- **Page 2+:** Details for the interested reader

## 2. Statement of Work (SOW) Template

### Full SOW Template

```markdown
# STATEMENT OF WORK

## [Project Title]

**Client:** [Company Name]  
**Freelancer:** [Your Name]  
**Date:** [Date]  
**SOW Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** [Start Date]  
**Project ID:** [Internal reference]

---

## 1. Project Overview

### 1.1 Background
[Client] is experiencing [specific pain point] which is costing 
approximately [quantified cost] in [time period]. Current solutions 
include [existing approach], which is insufficient because [specific 
shortcomings].

### 1.2 Objectives
This project aims to:
1. [Objective 1: measurable outcome]
2. [Objective 2: measurable outcome]
3. [Objective 3: measurable outcome]

### 1.3 Success Criteria
Success will be measured by:
- [Metric 1]: Target [X] within [timeframe]
- [Metric 2]: Target [Y] within [timeframe]
- [Metric 3]: Target [Z] within [timeframe]

---

## 2. Scope of Work

### 2.1 In Scope
The following work is included in this SOW:

**Phase 1: Discovery & Planning (Week 1)**
- Stakeholder interviews (up to 3 sessions)
- Current system audit and documentation
- Technical architecture design
- Project plan and milestone confirmation

**Phase 2: Development (Weeks 2-5)**
- Data pipeline development
- Model training and validation
- API development and testing
- Frontend dashboard (if applicable)

**Phase 3: Deployment & Handoff (Week 6)**
- Production deployment
- Monitoring setup
- Documentation delivery
- Training session (1 hour)

### 2.2 Out of Scope
The following work is NOT included and will require a separate SOW:
- [Excluded item 1: e.g., "Ongoing maintenance beyond 30 days"]
- [Excluded item 2: e.g., "Third-party API costs"]
- [Excluded item 3: e.g., "Data collection or labeling"]
- [Excluded item 4: e.g., "Major scope changes after sign-off"]

### 2.3 Assumptions
This SOW assumes:
- [Assumption 1: e.g., "Client will provide access to data within 3 days"]
- [Assumption 2: e.g., "Client will provide feedback within 48 hours"]
- [Assumption 3: e.g., "Existing infrastructure supports Docker deployment"]

## 6. Revisions & Change Management

### 6.1 Included Revisions
- 2 revision rounds per deliverable
- Revision requests must be submitted within 5 business days
- Revisions must be consistent with original SOW scope

### 6.2 Change Requests
- Changes to scope require written Change Request
- Changes may impact timeline and price
- Work on changes begins after approval of revised SOW
- Minimum change request billing: $[Amount]

## 7. Terms & Conditions

### 7.1 Intellectual Property
- Client owns all final deliverables upon full payment
- Freelancer retains rights to pre-existing IP and general methodologies
- Freelancer may use anonymized project details for portfolio/marketing

### 7.2 Confidentiality
- Both parties agree to NDA terms (attached if separate NDA exists)
- Client data handled per [GDPR/HIPAA/other] requirements

### 7.3 Cancellation
- Client may cancel within 5 days of kickoff for 50% refund
- After 5 days, deposit is non-refundable
- Freelancer may cancel with 14 days notice and pro-rata refund

### 7.4 Communication
- Primary channel: [Email/Slack/Discord]
- Response time: Within 24 hours (business days)
- Weekly status updates: [Day + time]
- Emergency contact: [Method for urgent issues]

### 7.5 Limitation of Liability
- Freelancer liability limited to total project fee
- Not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages
```

## 3. Mutual Agreement / Contract

### The Email-Based Mutual Agreement [Him Singh Style]

For smaller projects or when speed matters, a formal SOW can be simplified to an email-based mutual agreement:

```
Subject: [Client] x [Your Name] | Mutual Agreement for [Project]

Hi [Name],

So this is a mutual agreement cum contract for partnership between 
[Client] and us on delivering [specific work] as per your instructions.

Under this agreement we mutually agree on the following terms of deliverables:

We provide:
1) [Deliverable 1] — [Quantity/specification]
2) [Deliverable 2] — [Quantity/specification]
3) [Deliverable 3] — [Quantity/specification]
4) After the first draft there will be [X] revision rounds free of cost
5) After [X] revisions each revision round will cost an additional $[Amount]
6) Payments are to be made [upfront/for the work cycle/at milestones]
7) The styling/branding will be done according to [SOP/client guidelines]
8) We will review pricing after [time period]
9) You will provide us with [client responsibilities]

Kindly reply "YES" to confirm and agree.

If you have any questions or want to change something feel free to ask.

Cheers,
[Your name]
```

**Why it works:**
- Low friction (just reply "YES")
- Creates legal record (email is admissible)
- Fast (no DocuSign delays)
- Clear (bullet points, not legalese)

## 4. One-Page Proposal

### For Quick Bids or Small Projects

```markdown
# PROJECT PROPOSAL: [Title]

**For:** [Client Name] | **From:** [Your Name] | **Date:** [Date]

---

## The Challenge
[Client] is facing [specific pain] which costs approximately 
[quantified cost] per [time period].

## The Solution
I will [specific solution] which will [specific outcome].

## Deliverables
• [Deliverable 1]
• [Deliverable 2]
• [Deliverable 3]

## Timeline
Week 1: [Phase 1]  
Week 2-3: [Phase 2]  
Week 4: [Phase 3 — Delivery]

## Investment
**$[Amount]** — Fixed price, includes [X] revision rounds

**Payment:** 50% to start, 50% on delivery

## Next Step
Reply "YES" to this email to confirm, and I'll send the deposit 
invoice within 24 hours.

Questions? Just reply or book a quick call: [Calendly link]

---

[Your name] | [Title]  
[Email] | [Phone] | [Website]
```

## 6. AI Prompts

### Prompt 1: SOW Generator

```
Act as a contract specialist for freelance AI/ML projects.

Project details:
- Client: [COMPANY]
- Project: [TITLE]
- Scope: [WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING]
- Timeline: [WEEKS]
- Price: $[AMOUNT]
- Payment: [STRUCTURE]

Generate a complete SOW with:
1. Project overview (background + objectives + success criteria)
2. Scope (in-scope + out-of-scope + assumptions)
3. Deliverables table (5-7 items with acceptance criteria)
4. Timeline with milestones
5. Pricing and payment terms
6. Revision policy (2 rounds included, extras billed)
7. Terms (IP, confidentiality, cancellation, communication)
8. Signature block

Use professional but accessible language. No unnecessary legalese.
```

### Prompt 2: One-Page Proposal Writer

```
Act as a conversion copywriter for freelance proposals.

Discovery call insights:
- Client pain: [PAIN]
- Quantified cost: [$/month]
- Ideal outcome: [METRIC]
- Timeline: [WEEKS]
- Budget discussed: [RANGE]

Write a one-page proposal that:
1. Opens with their problem (not your credentials)
2. Presents solution in 2 sentences
3. Lists 3 specific deliverables
4. Shows timeline (4 weeks or less)
5. States price confidently (no justification)
6. Ends with "Reply YES" CTA

Keep under 300 words. Make it skimmable.
```

### Prompt 3: Change Request Handler

```
Act as a scope management expert.

Current SOW:
- Original scope: [WHAT WAS AGREED]
- Original price: $[AMOUNT]
- Original timeline: [WEEKS]

Client request:
- New ask: [WHAT THEY WANT]
- Impact on scope: [HOW IT CHANGES THINGS]

Draft a change request response:
1. Acknowledge the request positively
2. Explain impact on timeline
3. Explain impact on price
4. Offer alternatives (phased approach, reduced scope)
5. Ask for written confirmation to proceed

Tone: Collaborative, not defensive. Protect the relationship.
```
