# SOP: Growth Operations (Marketing, Leads, Sales)

This standard operating procedure governs how AGYOS executes growth tasks while preventing unconsented outreach, hallucinated testimonials, and unauthorized sales commitments.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Intake & Intent Verification**:
   - Extract campaign objective, target personas, and deliverable format.
   - Enforce R1 local reversible risk tier for draft copy and sales proposals.
   - If user requests live external broadcasting or unconsented email sends, flag as R3, stage in outbox, and require explicit approval.

2. **Audience & Provenance Research**:
   - Use verified public registries or approved customer segments.
   - Never scrape private directories or buy unverified contact lists.

3. **Draft Generation**:
   - Produce grounded, technical, human-language copy with zero AI buzzwords.
   - Base all ROI claims on actual documented data.

4. **Review & Hand-off**:
   - Deliver structured draft with clear approval boundaries.
   - Record snapshot ID in case of rollback.
