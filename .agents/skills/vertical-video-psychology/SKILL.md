# Vertical Video Psychology & Editing Standards

This skill codifies 116 empirical research principles across cognitive psychology, perception, multimedia learning, and audience telemetry into actionable instructions for editing vertical video (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and extracted clips).

---

## When to Activate This Skill

Activate this skill when:
- Scripting, storyboarding, or assembling vertical short-form video (9:16 format).
- Writing opening hooks, framing text overlays, or configuring on-screen safe zones.
- Synchronizing speech, audio beds, sound effects, and multi-language captions.
- Editing cuts, pacing event boundaries, or structuring technical tutorials.
- Reviewing video drafts against cognitive load, retention curves, and empirical metrics.

## Operational Editing Pipeline

Follow this 5-stage pipeline when producing or editing vertical video:

### Stage 1: The Opening Architecture (First 3 Seconds)
- Display the concrete noun and visible failure symptom in frame 1 [P02, P03].
- Establish the baseline, then introduce the single anomaly [P10].
- Eliminate generic throat-clearing, greetings, and ungrounded hyperbole [P01, P06].

### Stage 2: Dual-Track Safe-Zone Framing
- Confine critical UI, demonstration assets, and captions to the central 1080x1400 safe area, keeping 200px top and 320px bottom margins clear of overlays [P13].
- Write and verify the silent visual track first; ensure the full problem and solution are legible with volume muted [P24, P34].
- Crop dense desktop interfaces down to the active 4–6 lines; eliminate visual crowding [P12, P15].

### Stage 3: Audio & Caption Engineering
- Dialogue target: -14 LUFS integrated (-1.0 dB true peak) with zero room echo [P32].
- Background music: strictly instrumental during instructional sequences; no conflicting vocal tracks [P29].
- Captions: grouped into natural 2–4 word syntactic phrases with minimum 4.5:1 contrast backing [P22, P24]. Avoid single-word strobe tickers.

### Stage 4: Cognitive Pacing & Instruction
- Remove seductive details: strip unrelated bottom-screen gameplay or decorative b-roll [P49].
- Place labels directly adjacent to visual referents (spatial contiguity) [P47].
- Synchronize spoken names with the exact frame of physical action (temporal contiguity) [P48].
- Incorporate a 1-second pretesting pause before revealing the result [P57].

### Stage 5: Terminal Payoff & Call to Action
- Conclude on the working, verified state [P44].
- Frame the call to action as a conditional implementation intention: *"Next time your build fails on cache, check this environment variable"* [P103].
- Hold a clean 2-second reference card displaying the exact command or syntax for prospective memory retention [P114].
