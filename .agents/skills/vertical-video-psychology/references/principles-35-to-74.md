# Vertical Video Principles: 35 to 74

## Topic 4: Cuts, Excerpts, and Story Structure (Principles 35–45)

35. **Continuity Across a Cut**: Matching action vectors and gaze focal points prevents spatial disorientation across cuts.  
    *Edit Directive*: Cut on continuous physical motion (e.g., hand reaching toward a dial); ensure the angle and scale shift preserve spatial orientation. [R07]
36. **Event Boundaries**: Humans segment continuous experience into discrete events as goals, participants, or locations shift.  
    *Edit Directive*: Transition to a new shot when the workflow shifts (e.g., from diagnostic inspection to practical repair); hold the frame that confirms task success. [R08]
37. **Situation Models and Context**: Viewers continuously track participants, spatial coordinates, time, and intentions.  
    *Edit Directive*: In podcast or stream excerpts, establish the question and the speaker's identity before presenting the answer; retain the qualifications that define the answer's scope. [R40, V21]
38. **Causal Coherence**: Causal connectivity determines whether intermediate narrative steps are retained in memory.  
    *Edit Directive*: Show the fault state, the physical correction, and the operational outcome; verify that the intermediate step is visibly necessary for the resolution. [R38]
39. **Suspense**: Suspense is generated by establishing clear stakes combined with temporal uncertainty before the outcome.  
    *Edit Directive*: Demonstrate what is at risk before conducting the stress test; allow the result to resolve cleanly on-screen. [R39]
40. **Retrospective Curiosity**: Revealing an unexpected terminal outcome first creates an analytical search for cause.  
    *Edit Directive*: Open on the fractured mechanical component, then retrace the mechanical sequence that produced the fracture. [R39, R20]
41. **Juxtaposition and Context**: Facial expressions take their emotional interpretation from the preceding and surrounding context.  
    *Edit Directive*: Couple reaction shots directly with the triggering event; verify that cut context conveys the intended interpretation before finalizing. [R41]
42. **Narrative Transportation**: Immersion in a coherent story progression fosters deep cognitive engagement.  
    *Edit Directive*: Structure problem-solving around an individual navigating real-world constraints rather than reciting detached product features. [R36]
43. **Character Identification**: Viewers identify with individuals facing recognizable circumstances and limitations.  
    *Edit Directive*: Highlight relatable constraints (e.g. debugging a service with limited observability tooling or fixing a device using standard household gear). [R37]
44. **Remembered Peaks and Endings**: Retrospective evaluation is heavily weighted by the most intense moment and the final frame.  
    *Edit Directive*: Conclude the video on the demonstrable success state (e.g., the clear audio playback or fully functioning application). [R31]
45. **Serial Position Effects**: In listicle formats, primacy and recency dominate recall; middle items are frequently dropped.  
    *Edit Directive*: Place the most consequential instructional points at the start and conclusion; recap the crucial middle step before ending. [R87]

## Topic 5: Comprehension, Learning, and Memory (Principles 46–64)

46. **Working-Memory Load**: Cognitive capacity is bounded; novel elements compete for processing bandwidth.  
    *Edit Directive*: In multi-step commands, keep syntax references or terminal commands visible on-screen while narrating flags and parameters. [R10, R74]
47. **Spatial Contiguity**: Placing labels in immediate proximity to their physical referents eliminates split attention.  
    *Edit Directive*: Anchor metric annotations directly beside the active UI component or waveform peak rather than in a separate overlay box. [R09]
48. **Temporal Contiguity**: Synchronizing narration with physical demonstration prevents cognitive fragmentation.  
    *Edit Directive*: Align spoken directives ("disable hardware acceleration") with the exact millisecond the toggle state flips. [R09]
49. **Coherence and Seductive Details**: Extraneous stimuli (unrelated background gameplay, unrelated b-roll) impair skill acquisition.  
    *Edit Directive*: Strip away non-essential visual fluff and background gaming footage during technical instruction; maintain focus strictly on the task. [R27, R09]
50. **Dual Coding**: Complementary visual schematics and verbal explanations reinforce durable mental models.  
    *Edit Directive*: Present an illustrative architectural diagram while narrating data flow; avoid purely decorative stock imagery. [R88, R09]
51. **Pictorial Superiority**: Distinctive visual objects are recalled with greater fidelity than isolated verbal descriptions.  
    *Edit Directive*: Display the physical connector or UI layout explicitly rather than merely naming it in voiceover. [R89]
52. **Verbal Redundancy**: Redundant text streams (simultaneous spoken voiceover, verbatim paragraph text, and diagram labels) overload processing.  
    *Edit Directive*: Keep clean synchronized captions, but eliminate decorative paragraphs of duplicate text on-screen. [R09, R11, R12]
53. **Segmentation and Pacing**: Segmenting continuous procedures into discrete chunks supports mental integration.  
    *Edit Directive*: Insert clean visual chapter markers or natural pacing pauses after completing an operational step. [R09, R10]
54. **Pretraining**: Introducing component names and functions before showing complex interactions lowers cognitive friction.  
    *Edit Directive*: Show a static diagram explaining the audio gate and threshold before playing dynamic sound alterations. [R94]
55. **Worked Examples**: Step-by-step demonstrations reduce error search during early procedural acquisition.  
    *Edit Directive*: Demonstrate a complete edit detailing the rationale for each choice, then challenge the viewer with a partial exercise. [R10]
56. **Expertise Reversal**: Extensive instructional scaffolding that benefits beginners creates interference for advanced practitioners.  
    *Edit Directive*: Produce separate beginner-annotated and advanced high-density cuts tailored to specific viewer experience levels. [R10, V16]
57. **Pretesting and Prediction**: Prompting viewers to predict outcomes prior to revelation enhances subsequent retention.  
    *Edit Directive*: Freeze the frame before code executes; ask viewers to anticipate the console output, then run the snippet. [R24]
58. **Retrieval Practice**: Active retrieval attempts solidify memory representations.  
    *Edit Directive*: Conclude a tutorial by momentarily masking the key configuration parameter and prompting the audience to recall its name. [R25, R32]
59. **Spacing**: Distributing practice iterations over time establishes more resilient memory traces than massed repetition.  
    *Edit Directive*: Revisit a foundational concept in subsequent clips under a distinct application context. [R25]
60. **Self-Explanation**: Prompting learners to explain the underlying mechanism cements causal reasoning.  
    *Edit Directive*: Pose a diagnostic question: "Why did adding this capacitor remove the hum?" followed by the mechanical explanation. [R25, R26]
61. **Interleaving and Discrimination**: Alternating between contrasting problem categories improves differential diagnosis.  
    *Edit Directive*: Present clipped audio immediately adjacent to background hum to train discrimination between audio defects. [R25]
62. **Informative Feedback**: Specific corrective guidance is substantially more effective than generic pass/fail signals.  
    *Edit Directive*: Replace generic corrections with exact mechanistic feedback: "That slider adjusts volume; threshold sets when the gate opens." [R26]
63. **Isolation Effect (von Restorff)**: An item that differs distinctively from a uniform sequence receives preferential encoding.  
    *Edit Directive*: In a sequence of standard operations, isolate and visually emphasize the single edge case where the default rule fails. [R35]
64. **Feeling of Learning**: Subjective fluency during viewing does not guarantee objective operational competence.  
    *Edit Directive*: Test whether viewers can execute the demonstrated procedure on an unannotated interface rather than relying on self-reported ease. [R33, V16]

## Topic 6: Emotion, Humor, and Memory Priorities (Principles 65–74)

65. **Arousal and Transmission**: High physiological arousal states (such as awe or excitement) correlate with increased sharing behavior.  
    *Edit Directive*: Structure the demonstration around a genuine, high-contrast operational breakthrough rather than passive exposition. [R44]
66. **Arousal-Biased Competition**: Elevated arousal sharpens memory for high-priority central items while suppressing peripheral details.  
    *Edit Directive*: Display the single essential metric or parameter clearly on-screen before triggering high-energy demonstration sounds. [R46]
67. **Benign Violations**: Humor arises when an apparent violation or failure is simultaneously recognized as safe and benign.  
    *Edit Directive*: Allow an unexpected harmless mechanical failure or humorous prop to disrupt the workflow without compromising technical credibility. [R42]
68. **Humor Integration**: Humor enhances retention only when tightly tied to the core educational or product message.  
    *Edit Directive*: Make the product's actual mechanical characteristic central to the comic premise; avoid disconnected sketches. [R43]
69. **Threat and Efficacy**: Highlighting risk is effective only when paired immediately with a viable, concrete mitigation path.  
    *Edit Directive*: When showing a data-loss disaster, immediately demonstrate the single command required to restore the snapshot. [R47]
70. **Negativity and Selection**: Problem-centric phrasing captures attention, but requires precision to avoid deceptive sensationalism.  
    *Edit Directive*: Accurately frame real failure modes ("Why default Docker bridge settings leak ports") rather than relying on fabricated clickbait. [R91, R83]
71. **Nostalgia and Shared Experience**: Meaningful cultural references evoke social connection among cohorts familiar with them.  
    *Edit Directive*: Incorporate authentic era-specific computing or hardware cues when communicating with technical demographics. [R92, R72]
72. **Evaluative Conditioning**: Consistent pairing with a valence-laden stimulus gradually alters appraisal of an associated cue.  
    *Edit Directive*: Pair a subtle signature sonic motif with every successful project resolution across an episodic series. [R79]
73. **Processing Fluency**: High perceptual clarity facilitates positive evaluation, but must not substitute for empirical rigor.  
    *Edit Directive*: Ensure clean audio and legible layout while maintaining full visibility of raw data tables and technical benchmarks. [R29, R19]
74. **Social Comparison Sensitivity**: Depicting performance or capability demands realistic context regarding preparation and constraints.  
    *Edit Directive*: In technical workflow demonstrations, detail the prerequisite tools, time investment, and background setup required. [V17]
