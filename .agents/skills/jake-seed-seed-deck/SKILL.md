Turns the business plan into a 10-slide pitch deck in the order investors expect: title, problem, solution, market, competition, business model, go-to-market, team, financials, the ask. Writes ONE self-contained 16:9 HTML file (inline CSS, no fonts or scripts from the web, arrow-key navigation, a print stylesheet that saves to PDF one slide a page) in your brand colours, with real charts drawn from your numbers. Lints it: 35 words or fewer a slide, one idea a slide, a use of funds that adds up to the raise, and every number on a slide traced to your plan, model, market or competitor files. Reads seed/plan.json, writes seed/deck.html. Use when the user says \"make my pitch deck\", \"turn the plan into a deck\", \"build the slides\", \"check my deck\", \"how many words a slide\", or after /seed-plan."
