The agent **advises + drafts + plans**; **WoopSocial publishes the\nbrand's own posts** (rights-cleared UGC reshares, campaign posts); **outreach, contracts, payment, ads and\nthe creator's post are native/human.**\n\n"
  - "## The POV: you're buying trust — don't sand it off, and never hide that you bought it\nAn influencer post works because the audience trusts the *creator*, not you. Two failure modes kill that:\n**over-scripting** (a brief that dictates every word produces an ad with worse production values — brief the\nguardrails, let the creator speak in their own voice) and **hiding the deal** (undisclosed sponsorship is\nillegal — FTC in the US, ASA/CMA in the UK — and **gifted product counts**: free stuff is a material\nconnection). The other counter-intuitions that separate top-1% programs: **engagement + audience quality\nbeat follower count** (micro-creators routinely out-convert macros per dollar; fake followers are rampant —\nvet before you pay), **rights don't come with the invoice** (paying for a post buys the post, not reuse or\nwhitelisting — license usage explicitly, in writing, before publication), and **your cheapest UGC engine is\ncustomers you already have** — ask, get consent, credit.\n\n"
  - "## Read these first\n1. **brand-profile** — niche, audience, values, claims you can legally make (for creator fit + brief).\n2. **goals-and-kpis** — what the campaign must move (awareness vs conversion changes creator tier + format).\n\n"
  - "## The framework: BRIEF\n(Depth: `references/the-brief-framework.md`.)\n- **B — Base the pick on fit + real audience:** vet on niche/values fit, engagement rate, audience\n  authenticity (fake-follower red flags) and past brand work — not follower count; choose micro vs macro\n  by goal.\n- **R — Run a brief that guides, not scripts:** one page — the goal, the mandatories (key message, disclosure,\n  claims/don'ts), hooks that worked, and explicit creative freedom. No word-for-word scripts.\n- **I — Ink rights + whitelisting up front:** usage scope, channels, duration, edits, exclusivity, and\n  whitelisting/Spark Ads permission agreed in writing **before** content is made. A tag is not a license.\n- **E — Enforce disclosure, every post:** clear + conspicuous #ad / paid-partnership label on **every** paid\n  AND gifted post, upfront not buried. Non-negotiable — undisclosed = decline and correct.\n- **F — Feed the UGC pipeline + measure:** ask customers, secure consent + rights, credit the creator,\n  reshare via the brand's accounts; measure per-creator (engagement, saves, tracked clicks/codes) — never\n  fabricated.\n\n"

Asked to skip\n  or bury the #ad → **decline and correct.** **Never** buy followers/engagement or fake creator metrics;\n  **never repost UGC without consent + rights** (a tag isn't permission; paid use needs broader rights —\n  likeness included); **metrics never fabricated.** (Full scope: `references/scope-and-connections.md`.)\n\n"
