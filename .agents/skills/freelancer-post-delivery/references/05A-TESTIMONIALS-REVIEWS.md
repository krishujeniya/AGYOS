## 1. The 3-Ask Testimonial Method

### The Timeline

| Ask | Timing | Method | Expected Yes Rate | Purpose |
|-----|--------|--------|-------------------|---------|
| **Ask 1** | At delivery (within 48 hours) | In-person or video call | 60-70% | Capture enthusiasm while fresh |
| **Ask 2** | At 30 days | Email with metrics | 50-60% | Validate long-term results |
| **Ask 3** | At 90 days | Email + LinkedIn | 30-40% | Request referral intro |

### Why the 3-Ask Works

1. **At delivery:** Client is excited, project is fresh, emotions are high
2. **At 30 days:** Client has seen results, can speak to impact
3. **At 90 days:** Client trusts you, knows your work, willing to vouch

### The Escalation of Ask

```
Ask 1 (Delivery): "Quick testimonial?" → Low friction
Ask 2 (30 days): "Case study feature?" → Medium commitment
Ask 3 (90 days): "Referral intro?" → High trust required
```

## 2. Testimonial Formats

### Format 1: Video Testimonial (Most Powerful)

**Specifications:**
| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Length | 1-2 minutes |
| Quality | Phone camera acceptable, good lighting |
| Setting | Client's office or natural environment |
| Questions | 4 scripted prompts |
| Editing | Minimal (trim start/end, add logo) |

**The 4 Questions to Ask:**
1. "What problem were you facing before working with us?"
2. "What did we build/solve for you?"
3. "What specific result did you achieve? (with numbers if possible)"
4. "Would you recommend us to others?"

**Recording Tips:**
- Send questions 24 hours in advance
- Offer to record via Zoom (you control the file)
- Provide Loom link as easy alternative
- Give them 3 takes (no pressure)

### Format 2: Written Testimonial

**Template for Client:**
```
"Before working with [Your Name], we were struggling with [specific pain].

They built [specific solution] which [specific outcome].

The result: [quantified metric].

I would highly recommend [Your Name] to any [target client] looking 
to [specific outcome]."
```

**Where to Use:**
- Website homepage
- Proposal appendices
- LinkedIn recommendations
- Upwork/Contra reviews
- Email signatures

### Format 3: Case Study (Most Detailed)

**Structure:** STAR-M (Situation, Task, Action, Result, Metrics)

See Module 01C for full case study template.

### Format 4: Social Proof Screenshot

**What to Screenshot:**
- Slack/Teams praise messages
- Email thank-you notes
- LinkedIn endorsements
- Payment confirmations with nice notes
- Post-project survey responses

**Where to Post:**
- LinkedIn carousel
- Instagram stories
- Twitter/X threads
- Website social proof section

## 3. Collection Scripts

### Script 1: At Delivery (Video)

```
"Hey [Name], I'm so glad we got this across the finish line!

Would you do me a quick favor? I'm building my portfolio and 
would love a 1-2 minute video from you about the project.

Nothing fancy — just your phone camera, good light, and honest 
thoughts on:
1. What problem you had
2. What we built
3. What result you got

I can send you the questions in advance, or we can hop on a 
quick Zoom and I'll record it for you. Whatever's easier.

No pressure at all — just thought I'd ask while the project's 
fresh in your mind!

Thanks,
[Your name]"
```

### Script 2: At 30 Days (Written + Metrics)

```
Subject: Quick check-in + favor?

Hi [Name],

Hope [system/project] is running smoothly! It's been 30 days 
since launch.

Quick questions:
1. How are the metrics looking? Any wins you've noticed?
2. Is the team finding it useful?
3. Any tweaks or questions?

Also — if you're happy with the results, would you mind writing 
a brief testimonial? Just 2-3 sentences about your experience.

Even better: if you have any metrics you can share (anonymized 
is fine), I'd love to feature your success in a case study.

Either way, happy to jump on a quick call if helpful.

Best,
[Your name]"
```

### Script 3: At 90 Days (Referral)

```
Subject: Who else should know about this?

Hi [Name],

It's been 3 months since we launched [project], and I hope 
it's still delivering value for [Company].

Quick ask: do you know anyone in your network who might be 
facing similar [pain point] challenges?

I'm looking for 2-3 more [type of company] to work with this 
quarter, and a warm intro from you would mean the world.

As a thank you, I'll [incentive: $500 credit, free month of 
support, free audit, etc.] for any referral that becomes a client.

No worries at all if no one comes to mind — just thought I'd ask!

Best,
[Your name]"
```

## 4. Display Strategy

### The Testimonial Portfolio

| Location | Format | Purpose |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Website homepage** | 3 video testimonials above fold | Immediate trust |
| **Services page** | Relevant testimonial per service | Contextual proof |
| **Case studies** | Embedded quote + video | Deep credibility |
| **LinkedIn Featured** | Screenshot + link | Profile trust |
| **Proposals** | 1 relevant testimonial | Close deals |
| **Email signature** | Rotating quote | Passive proof |
| **Upwork/Contra** | 5-star reviews | Platform ranking |

### The Testimonial Wall

Create a dedicated page on your website:

```

"[Quote]"

[Written 2: Client D]
"[Quote]"
— [Name], [Title], [Company]

[Case Study Link: Client E]
"Full case study: How we saved $24K/year"
```

## 5. Review Generation

### Platform Review Strategy

| Platform | When to Ask | How to Ask | Impact |
|----------|-------------|------------|--------|
| **Upwork** | At project close | "Mind leaving a quick review?" | JSS score, visibility |
| **Contra** | At project close | "Would love your feedback on Contra" | Profile ranking |
| **LinkedIn** | Anytime | "Would you write me a recommendation?" | Profile credibility |
| **Google** | After 30 days | "Would you leave a Google review?" | Local SEO |
| **Clutch** | Enterprise clients | "Would you submit a review on Clutch?" | B2B credibility |
| **G2** | SaaS/tool projects | "Would you review us on G2?" | Product credibility |

### The Review Request Email

```
Subject: Quick favor — 2 minutes?

Hi [Name],

Working with you on [project] was a highlight of my quarter.

If you have 2 minutes, would you leave a review on [platform]? 
It really helps other [target clients] find me.

[Direct link to review page]

No pressure at all — just thought I'd ask!

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

## 6. AI Prompts

### Prompt 1: Testimonial Request Writer

```
Act as a client success manager.

Client details:
- Name: [NAME]
- Company: [COMPANY]
- Project: [WHAT YOU BUILT]
- Result: [QUANTIFIED OUTCOME]
- Days since delivery: [X]

Write 3 testimonial request messages:
1. Day-of-delivery request (casual, immediate)
2. 30-day check-in (metrics-focused, case study angle)
3. 90-day referral ask (warm intro request)

Each must:
- Reference specific project details
- Make it easy to say yes (low friction)
- Include an incentive or thank-you
- Sound personal, not templated
```

### Prompt 2: Testimonial Editor

```
Act as a copy editor for client testimonials.

Raw testimonial:
"[PASTE RAW TESTIMONIAL]"

Edit to:
1. Lead with the problem (first 10 words)
2. Include specific outcome (with numbers)
3. End with recommendation
4. Keep under 50 words
5. Maintain client's voice and authenticity

Provide 3 versions:
- Short (25 words)
- Medium (40 words)
- Long (75 words)
```

### Prompt 3: Case Study from Testimonial

```
Act as a case study writer.

Testimonial content:
"[PASTE TESTIMONIAL]"

Project details:
- Client: [COMPANY]
- Industry: [INDUSTRY]
- Duration: [WEEKS]
- Tech stack: [TOOLS]

Expand into a mini case study (300 words):
1. Situation (2 sentences)
2. Task (1 sentence)
3. Action (2-3 bullets)
4. Result (with metrics)
5. Quote (from testimonial)

Make it ready for website publication.
```

## 7. Action Checklist

### At Delivery (Within 48 Hours)

- [ ] Send testimonial request (video preferred)
- [ ] Send Upwork/Contra review request (if applicable)
- [ ] Screenshot any praise from Slack/email
- [ ] Add project to portfolio
- [ ] Schedule 30-day check-in

### At 30 Days

- [ ] Send check-in email with metrics request
- [ ] Ask for written testimonial
- [ ] Ask for case study participation
- [ ] Request LinkedIn recommendation
- [ ] Update case study with results

### At 90 Days

- [ ] Send referral request
- [ ] Ask for introduction to 2-3 contacts
- [ ] Offer referral incentive
- [ ] Update website with new testimonial
- [ ] Share testimonial on social media

### Ongoing

- [ ] Maintain testimonial tracker (who, when, format, status)
- [ ] Rotate testimonials on website quarterly
- [ ] Add testimonials to proposals
- [ ] Request 1 new testimonial per month
