  - "# TikTok Growth\n\nThis is the TikTok-specific **growth system** — not a content skill. It diagnoses *where* growth is\nstuck and routes to the skills that fix it, on top of how TikTok actually distributes content in\n2026. Don't carry over Instagram assumptions; TikTok is a different machine.\n\nThree truths shape everything:\n\n1. **TikTok is an interest graph, not a social graph.** Follower count is **not a ranking factor** —\n   every video is tested on a cold audience, and ~70–80% of the FYP is non-followers. **You don't\n   grow by accumulating followers for reach.**\n2. **Completion + rewatch win.** The top signal is **completion rate (aim ~70%+)**, then\n   **rewatches/loops**; likes are the weakest. The first ~1 second decides everything.\n3. **Search and volume are growth pillars.** TikTok is a search engine (evergreen discovery), and\n   each post is a cheap **at-bat** — consistency + reading patterns beats one-off luck.\n\nFollowers are the **business** asset (LIVE/Shop/owned audience), not the reach mechanism. (Full\nmodel: `references/the-growth-engine.md`.)\n\n"
  - "## Step 0 — Read the foundation + the current state\n\nLoad `brand-profile.md`, `social-strategy.md`, `audience.md`. Get the **goal** and the **current\nstate** from WoopSocial post analytics (views, likes, comments, shares, saves) plus native TikTok\nanalytics for completion %, rewatches, traffic source, and follows-per-video — those are not stored\nin WoopSocial. Never fabricate.\n\n"
  - "## Step 1 — Diagnose the broken stage/phase\n\nFind where it's stuck in the **test-and-expand loop**: Phase-1 death (hook/completion) → Phase-2\nplateau (cold-audience hold) → follow-conversion → search → volume/consistency → niche → community. A\ngrowth problem is almost always **one** stage. See the diagnosis table in\n`references/features-and-diagnosis.md`.\n\n"
  - "## Step 2 — Fix completion + rewatch (the dominant signal)\n\nIf reach is the issue: rebuild the **1-second hook**, raise **completion toward ~70%+** (fast pacing,\nno dead air, right length), and add a **loop/rewatch trigger**. → `tiktok-script`, `hook-writer`. See\n`references/completion-search-volume.md`.\n\n"
  - "## Step 3 — Build the search lane (evergreen discovery)\n\nPut the **primary keyword in spoken audio + on-screen text + caption** (the triple mention), write\ncaptions like search queries, and build keyword clusters so videos get found for months independent\nof the FYP. → `hashtag-strategy`/`social-seo`, `tiktok-script`.\n\n"
  - "## Step 4 — Run consistent at-bats + read patterns\n\nA sustainable cadence (commonly ~3–5×/week, quality-gated) gives the algorithm more tests; **judge by\npatterns across many videos, not one flop**; the back-catalog resurfaces. **Photo Mode carousels** are\ncheap extra at-bats where the niche fits (high-ceiling, niche-dependent — not a reach hack) →\n`tiktok-photo-mode`. → `content-calendar`, `viral-reverse-engineering`. See\n`references/completion-search-volume.md`.\n\n"
  - "## Step 5 — Convert + retain with native features\n\n- **Convert:** if views are high but follows are low, it's the **profile** + no reason to follow →\n  `profile-optimization` + a **Series/Parts** (drives binge + follows).\n- **Reach features:** **Stitch/Duet** to piggyback momentum; **pinned comment** to seed engagement.\n- **Community/business:** **LIVE** (needs ~1k followers), playlists, video replies — for warm fans,\n  not early reach. See `references/features-and-diagnosis.md`.\n\n"
  - "## Step 6 — Niche, cadence, measurement\n\n- **Tighten the niche** so the interest graph can categorize and recommend you (\"sides of TikTok\").\n- **Sustainable cadence** → `content-calendar`.\n- **Measure** via native analytics (completion, rewatch, shares, traffic source, follows-per-video).\n  No WoopSocial analytics; **follower count isn't the goal metric.**\n\n"
  - "## Orchestration map\n\ntiktok-growth diagnoses and routes; these execute: `tiktok-script` · `tiktok-photo-mode` ·\n`hook-writer` · `caption-writer` · `profile-optimization` · `content-calendar` · `hashtag-strategy` ·\n`trend-jacking` · `viral-reverse-engineering` · `cross-platform-repurposing` · `scheduling-and-queue`.\n\n"
  - "## Quality bar — self-check\n\n- Did I **diagnose the broken stage/phase** rather than spray generic tips?\n- Did I center **completion (~70%+) + rewatch** as the signals, and the **1-second hook**?\n- Did I build the **search lane** and frame growth as **consistent at-bats + patterns**?\n- Did I treat **followers as a business asset**, not a reach lever?\n- Did I route to the right skills, with **sustainable cadence** and **native-analytics** measurement?\n- **No bought followers / no #fyp / no polished-ad advice / no guarantees**, and the **flux** caveat?\n\n"
  - "## Edge cases & pushback\n\n- **\"Buy followers / I need followers for reach\"** → refuse fake engagement; follower count isn't a\n  ranking factor; reframe to completion/search + followers-as-business-asset.\n- **\"~200 views then dead\"** → Phase-1 death → hook + completion → `tiktok-script`/`hook-writer`.\n- **\"One flop, the algorithm hates me\"** → single videos are noise; consistent at-bats + read\n  patterns.\n- **\"Guarantee me viral\"** → no guarantees; stack the odds; note the 2026 algorithm flux.\n- **Scattered niche** → tighten the lane so the interest graph can categorize you.\n- **\"Use LIVE to grow\"** (small account) → LIVE needs ~1k and is community/monetization, not early\n  reach.\n- **\"Which video performs best?\"** → native TikTok analytics; no WoopSocial analytics.\n\n"
