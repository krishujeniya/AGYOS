## 1. Profile Architecture

### The 7-Second Test

When a prospect lands on your profile, they decide in 7 seconds:

| Question | Element | Goal |
|----------|---------|------|
| "Is this person relevant?" | Headline | Match their search intent |
| "Do they look professional?" | Photo + Banner | Build instant trust |
| "Can they solve my problem?" | About Section | Communicate transformation |
| "Do others trust them?" | Featured + Activity | Social proof |
| "What have they built?" | Experience | Quantified outcomes |

## 2. Banner & Visual Identity

### Banner Specifications

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 1584 x 396 px |
| Format | JPG or PNG (under 8MB) |
| Content | Value proposition + social proof + CTA |
| Tool | Canva Pro (free tier sufficient) |

### Banner Copy Formula

```
Line 1: [PRIMARY SKILL] | [SECONDARY SKILL] | [PROOF POINT]
Line 2: [CTA: "Let's build your AI system →"]
```

### Example Banners

**AI/ML Freelancer:**
```
AI/ML Engineer | Full-Stack Developer | MLOps Specialist
3+ Enterprise Clients | Production Systems | DM "PROJECT" to Start
```

**Full-Stack Developer:**
```
Full-Stack AI Developer | React • FastAPI • GCP
Shipped 5 Production Apps | 100% On-Time Delivery | Book Free Call
```

**Data Scientist:**
```
Predictive Analytics | MLOps | Business Intelligence
$2M+ Revenue Impact for Clients | Let's Turn Your Data Into Dollars
```

### Profile Photo Specifications

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Dimensions | 400 x 400 px minimum |
| Style | Professional headshot, eye contact |
| Background | Solid color or subtle gradient |
| Expression | Confident, approachable, competent |
| Tool | pfpmaker.com (AI-enhanced professional photos) |

### The 3-Second Trust Test

Look at your photo and ask:
- Does this person look **competent**? (Technical but approachable)
- Does this person look **reliable**? (Consistent, professional)
- Does this person look **credible**? (Confident, experienced)

If any answer is "no," retake the photo.

## 6. Content Strategy

### 2026 Algorithm Priorities

| Priority | Metric | Why It Matters |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **#1** | Dwell time | Longer reads = more reach |
| **#2** | Comments | Engagement quality > quantity |
| **#3** | Consistency | Daily > sporadic blockbuster |
| **#4** | Shares | Extended network reach |
| **#5** | Likes | Vanity metric, least important |

### The 4-1-1 Content Rule

| Type | Frequency | Purpose | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Educational** | 4x/week | Build authority | "3 MLOps mistakes killing your model in production" |
| **Proof of Work** | 1x/week | Social proof | "Client X just hit $50K MRR using our churn prediction" |
| **Personal/Behind-the-Scenes** | 1x/week | Humanize brand | "How I debugged a production model at 2 AM" |

```
[PROBLEM - Relatable pain point with data]
[SOLUTION - Your unique approach]
[PROOF - Screenshot, metric, or client result]
[CTA - Engagement trigger]
```

### Ideal Prospect Criteria

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Title** | CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Data, Founder, CEO, Product Lead |
| **Company** | B2B SaaS, Series A-C startups, Enterprise IT, Digital Agencies |
