Lays out the nine standard folders (01 Company, 02 Pitch deck, 03 Financials, 04 Market, 05 Product, 06 Team, 07 Legal, 08 Cap table, 09 Customers), checks the files against a 25-item checklist (present, missing, or stale by the date in the file name or its last change), writes seed/dataroom/INDEX.md describing every file, and lists what to share before a term sheet and what only after one. Flags what must never be in a data room: passwords, keys, ID documents, raw customer lists. It never uploads or shares anything. Use when the user says \"build my data room\", \"what goes in a data room\", \"what am I missing for diligence\", \"the investor asked for the data room\", \"what can I share now\", \"make an index of my documents\", or after a /seed-closer call goes well or /seed-terms reads a term sheet."
