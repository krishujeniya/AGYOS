# AI industry watchlist

Use the supplied directory as a starting list, not as proof that an account is current or official.

## Workflow

1. Read `docs/references/industry/AI_Accounts_To_Follow.txt` and keep the original PDF at `docs/references/industry/AI_Accounts_To_Follow.pdf` as the source record.
2. Ask which platforms and topics matter if the user wants a personalized list. Keep the initial list small enough to follow consistently.
3. Verify each selected organization or person's identity from an official site or another first-party source. Link that evidence and record the checked date; do not guess handles from display names.
4. Separate first-party announcements from community discussion. Label unaffiliated Reddit communities as community sources.
5. Present a compact table with organization/person, topic, platform, verified profile link, source, and checked date. Mark unavailable profiles as unknown rather than inventing one.
6. Refresh unstable account links and organizational roles before recommending them as current.

## Boundaries

- Do not sign into accounts, follow profiles, enable notifications, or change a feed without the user's explicit request.
- Do not claim that a small watchlist is comprehensive or that a source is unbiased.
- Keep the PDF's statements and handles attributed to that supplied edition; newer claims need current first-party evidence.
