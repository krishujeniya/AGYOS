Your first 500 paying users, without ads.
The creator-comments playbook from the video, with five prompts you can copy into Claude or ChatGPT.
Checked 4 Nov 2026.

1. The idea
People who already want your product are telling each other so, in the comments under creators they follow.
You read those comments, make one useful short video per complaint, and show your app as the fix. You
borrow trust from an audience that already exists instead of paying to rent attention.

3. The maths, and what it is not
20 creators × 25 paying users each = 500. That is a target to work towards, not a guarantee. Your numbers
depend on your product, your price and how good the videos are. Measure signups and payments per video,
then double down on what converts.

 Do it clean. Do not drop links under creators' posts or spam their comments. Use the comments as research, post
 on your own accounts, and tag or collaborate only when a creator agrees. The ThaliFit app in our video is an
 invented example.
                                                                                                         THE FIVE PROMPTS



Prompt 1: find the 20 creators
  I sell [APP] to [CUSTOMER] in [COUNTRY]. Their main problem is [PROBLEM].
  List the topics and search terms these people follow on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.
  Then give me a checklist to judge a creator with 5K to 50K followers: audience fit, comment
  quality, posting frequency.
  Do not invent creator names. I will search the terms myself and fill in a sheet.



Prompt 2: mine the complaints
  Below are real comments from top posts in my niche. Group them into complaint themes.
  For each theme give: a short name, how many comments fit it, 3 exact quotes, and what the person
  really wants.
  Rank the themes by how often they appear and how strongly people say they would pay.
  Use only these comments. If something is not in the data, say so.


  [PASTE COMMENTS]



Prompt 3: write the 50 to 80 second video
  Write a [LANGUAGE] short video script, 50 to 80 seconds, about this complaint: "[EXACT QUOTE]".
  Hook: use the viewer's own words in the first line.
  Middle: 3 specific tips that help even if they never use my app.
  End: show how [APP] solves it in one clear step, then one call to action.
  Do not make claims I cannot prove. Mark anything I must verify.



Prompt 4: check the offer before you post
  Here is my landing page text and price: [PASTE].
  A viewer arrives from a video about "[COMPLAINT]". Does the first screen answer that complaint?
  List the 5 biggest reasons they would leave without paying, and rewrite the headline and button
  text.



Prompt 5: read the results weekly
  Here is my weekly data per video: views, profile visits, signups, payments. [PASTE]
  Tell me which complaint theme converts best per 1,000 views, which video to remake, and which to
  stop.
  Give me next week's 3 videos. Do not treat views alone as success.
