## Agent Guidelines & Workflows

### 1. Outbound Prospecting & Lead Gen
When asked to build lists or identify potential leads:
- Reference [02A-PROSPECTING-SYSTEM.md](references/02A-PROSPECTING-SYSTEM.md) to set up filters on Apollo.io and LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
- Implement **Signal-Based Prospecting**: focus on target companies that recently raised funding, hired new executives, or published job postings in target technology stacks.
- Guide the freelancer to monitor Reddit and communities for direct problem signals.

### 2. Cold Outreach Execution
When drafting cold emails, DMs, or follow-up sequences:
- Reference [02B-COLD-OUTREACH.md](references/02B-COLD-OUTREACH.md) to apply the **Ultra-Short Value Offer** format (40–60 words, personalized hook, specific outcome proof, low-friction call-to-action).
- Structure follow-up campaigns using the 5-email sequence: Day 1 (Value Offer) $\rightarrow$ Day 3 (Case Study snippet) $\rightarrow$ Day 7 (Resource/Quick win) $\rightarrow$ Day 10 (Direct question) $\rightarrow$ Day 14 (Breakup email).

### 3. Freelancing Platform Optimization
When asked to optimize freelancer profiles or proposal strategies on platforms:
- Reference [02C-PLATFORM-STRATEGY.md](references/02C-PLATFORM-STRATEGY.md) to structure profiles for SEO and JSS (Job Success Score) maintenance on Upwork.
- Compare Upwork's commission model with Contra's flat-fee model ($29/contract) to guide pricing and platform selection.

### 4. Content Marketing & Organic Traffic
When writing LinkedIn posts or building content plans:
- Reference [02D-CONTENT-MARKETING.md](references/02D-CONTENT-MARKETING.md) to write hooks and body copy optimized for **LinkedIn Dwell Time** (dwell time $>$ simple likes).
- Leverage the content repurposing flywheel to break down one long-form asset (case study, technical article) into 10 high-impact social posts.

### 5. Referral loops
When establishing customer retention and referral systems:
- Reference [02E-REFERRAL-ENGINE.md](references/02E-REFERRAL-ENGINE.md) to execute the **3-Ask Testimonial Method** (ask at kickoff, ask at delivery, ask 30 days post-delivery).
- Establish double-sided referral incentives and strategic agency partnerships.
