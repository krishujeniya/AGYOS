# 03A: Discovery Call System

> **Core Principle:** The discovery call is not a sales pitch. It is a diagnostic interview. Your job is to understand their pain so deeply that they convince themselves they need you.
>
> *"Hey, can we schedule a quick Zoom call to discuss everything in detail?"* — Him Singh

## 1. Pre-Call Setup

### Booking Page Configuration

**Tools:** Calendly, TidyCal, Acuity Scheduling

**Calendly Settings:**
| Setting | Recommendation | Why |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| **Duration** | 30 minutes | Long enough to diagnose, short enough to respect time |
| **Buffer** | 15 min before, 15 min after | Prep and debrief time |
| **Availability** | Your working hours in client's timezone | Reduces friction |
| **Questions** | 3 pre-call questions | Qualifies before the call |
| **Reminder** | 24-hour + 1-hour email + SMS | Reduces no-shows |
| **Brand** | Your logo, colors, custom URL | Professional impression |

**Pre-Call Questions (Calendly Custom Fields):**
```
1. What specific challenge are you looking to solve? (required)
2. What's your ideal timeline for this project? (required)
3. Have you worked with a freelancer/agency before on similar work? (optional)
```

### Pre-Call Research Checklist

Before every call, spend 10 minutes researching:

- [ ] Company website (products, team, recent news)
- [ ] LinkedIn company page (hiring, posts, growth)
- [ ] Crunchbase (funding, investors, competitors)
- [ ] Their current tech stack (BuiltWith, Wappalyzer)
- [ ] Recent press releases or blog posts
- [ ] Competitor landscape (2-3 key competitors)

### Pre-Call Mindset

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| Pitch your services | Diagnose their pain |
| Talk 80% of the time | Listen 80% of the time |
| Quote prices early | Understand value first |
| Promise everything | Set realistic expectations |
| Rush to close | Build trust slowly |

## 2. Call Qualification Framework

### BANT+ for Freelancers

| Criteria | Question to Ask | Red Flag |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| **Budget** | "What budget range have you allocated for this?" | "We don't have a budget yet" |
| **Authority** | "Who else needs to approve this decision?" | "I need to check with my boss" (no access to decision-maker) |
| **Need** | "What happens if this problem isn't solved in 6 months?" | "It's not really urgent" |
| **Timeline** | "When do you need this delivered?" | "No specific deadline" |
| **+ Fit** | "What does success look like for you?" | Vague, unmeasurable goals |
| **+ Pain** | "How is this problem affecting revenue/operations today?" | Can't quantify the pain |

### The 3-Minute Opening

**Minute 1: Rapport**
```
"Thanks for making time, [Name]. Before we dive in, I did some homework 
on [Company] — [specific observation from research]. That's impressive 
growth. How long have you been with the team?"
```

**Minute 2: Agenda Setting**
```
"Here's how I'd love to use our 30 minutes:
1. First, understand your current situation and what's not working
2. Then, explore what 'great' would look like for you
3. Finally, if it makes sense, I'll share how I approach similar challenges

Does that work for you? Anything you want to make sure we cover?"
```

**Minute 3: Permission to Ask Hard Questions**
```
"I'm going to ask some direct questions about [pain area] — not to 
embarrass anyone, but to make sure I understand the real scope. 
Is that okay?"
```

## 3. The 7-Question Discovery Script

### Question 1: The Current State
```
"Walk me through how you [handle this process] today. 
What does that look like from start to finish?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Manual steps that could be automated
- Tools they're using (or not using)
- Who else is involved in the process
- Where things break down

### Question 2: The Pain Point
```
"Where does that process break down or cause the most friction? 
What takes the most time or creates the most headaches?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Specific pain (not vague complaints)
- Frequency of the pain (daily? weekly?)
- Who feels the pain (them? team? customers?)
- Cost of the pain (time, money, opportunity)

### Question 3: The Impact
```
"If you had to put a number on it, how much is this costing you 
per month? In time, money, or missed opportunity?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Can they quantify the pain?
- Is the number big enough to justify your fee?
- Do they realize the full cost?

### Question 4: The Attempted Solutions
```
"What have you already tried to solve this? What worked and what didn't?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Previous freelancer/agency experience
- In-house attempts (and why they failed)
- Budget already spent on failed solutions
- What they're NOT willing to try again

### Question 5: The Ideal Outcome
```
"If we were to check back in 90 days and this project was a massive 
success, what would have changed? What metrics would be different?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Measurable success criteria
- Realistic expectations
- Timeline for results
- Who defines "success"

### Question 6: The Decision Process
```
"Assuming we decide there's a good fit today, what would the next 
steps look like on your end? Who else needs to be involved?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Decision-making process
- Number of stakeholders
- Approval timeline
- Potential blockers

### Question 7: The Investment Mindset
```
"Projects like this typically require an investment of [range]. 
Is that in line with what you were expecting?"
```

**What you're listening for:**
- Budget reality check
- Price sensitivity
- Room for negotiation
- Decision urgency

## 4. Call Notes & Documentation

### The Discovery Call Notes Template

```markdown

## Basic Info
- **Company:** [Name]
- **Contact:** [Name, Title]
- **Call Date:** [Date]
- **Duration:** [Minutes]
- **Source:** [How they found you]

## Current State
- **Process:** [How they do it today]
- **Tools:** [What they use]
- **Team:** [Who's involved]

## Pain Points
1. [Pain 1] — [Frequency] — [Cost]
2. [Pain 2] — [Frequency] — [Cost]
3. [Pain 3] — [Frequency] — [Cost]

## Impact Quantification
- **Time cost:** [Hours/month]
- **Money cost:** [$/month]
- **Opportunity cost:** [What's missed]
- **Total estimated cost:** [$X/month or $Y/year]

## Attempted Solutions
- [Solution 1] — [Result]
- [Solution 2] — [Result]

## Ideal Outcome (90-Day Success)
- **Metric 1:** [Target]
- **Metric 2:** [Target]
- **Metric 3:** [Target]

## Decision Process
- **Decision maker:** [Name/Title]
- **Other stakeholders:** [Names]
- **Approval timeline:** [Weeks]
- **Budget range:** [$X - $Y]
- **Expected start date:** [Date]

## My Assessment
- **Fit score:** [1-10]
- **Red flags:** [Any concerns]
- **Recommended approach:** [Starter/Pro/Enterprise]
- **Estimated value:** [10-20% of their quantified pain]
- **Proposed price:** [$Z]

## Next Steps
- [ ] Send proposal by [date]
- [ ] Include: [specific items]
- [ ] Follow up on: [date]
```

## 5. Post-Call Actions

### The 24-Hour Rule

Within 24 hours of every discovery call:

| Action | Time | Purpose |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Send thank-you email** | 2 hours | Gratitude + recap |
| **Send proposal** | 24 hours | Strike while interest is hot |
| **Calendar follow-up** | Immediate | Don't let it slip |

### Thank-You Email Template

```
Subject: Great chatting, [Name] — here's what I heard

Hi [Name],

Thanks for making time today. I enjoyed learning about [Company]'s 
challenges around [specific pain point].

To recap what I heard:
• [Pain point 1] is costing you approximately [quantified cost]
• [Pain point 2] is creating friction for [team/customers]
• Your ideal 90-day outcome is [specific metric]

Based on our conversation, I believe the [Starter/Pro/Enterprise] 
approach would be the best fit. I'm putting together a detailed 
proposal with scope, timeline, and investment — you'll have it 
by [day + time].

In the meantime, if any questions come up, just reply to this email 
or DM me on LinkedIn.

Looking forward to potentially working together.

Best,
[Your name]
[Portfolio link]
[Calendly link]
```

## 6. AI Prompts

### Prompt 1: Discovery Call Prep

```
Act as a sales coach for freelance developers.

I have a discovery call with:
- Company: [COMPANY]
- Contact: [NAME, TITLE]
- Industry: [INDUSTRY]
- Known pain: [PAIN POINT]

Create:
1. 5 custom discovery questions based on their industry
2. 3 potential objections they might raise
3. 2 relevant case studies to reference
4. 1 pricing anchor to test (value-based, not hourly)
5. A 30-minute agenda (minute-by-minute)

Make it specific to their situation. No generic advice.
```

### Prompt 2: Call Notes Analyzer

```
Act as a sales analyst.

Here are my raw discovery call notes:
[PASTE NOTES]

Analyze and provide:
1. Fit score (1-10) with reasoning
2. Recommended service tier (Starter/Pro/Enterprise)
3. Suggested pricing (value-based calculation)
4. Red flags or concerns
5. Specific proposal angle
6. Follow-up email draft

Be direct and honest. If it's a bad fit, say so.
```

### Prompt 3: Objection Predictor

```
Act as a sales psychologist.

Based on this discovery call context:
- Client type: [TYPE]
- Budget mentioned: [RANGE]
- Previous experience: [GOOD/BAD/NONE]
- Decision makers: [NUMBER]
- Timeline: [URGENT/FLEXIBLE]

Predict the top 5 objections they'll raise when seeing the proposal.
For each, provide:
1. The objection (exact words they might use)
2. Why they're really saying it (underlying fear)
3. Your response (under 50 words)
4. A question to redirect them
```

## 7. Action Checklist

### Before Your First Call

- [ ] Set up Calendly/TidyCal with custom branding
- [ ] Write 3 pre-call questions
- [ ] Create call notes template (copy from above)
- [ ] Practice the 7-question script with a friend
- [ ] Set up recording (with permission) or note-taking system

### After Every Call

- [ ] Fill out call notes template within 30 minutes
- [ ] Send thank-you email within 2 hours
- [ ] Calculate value-based pricing (10-20% of quantified pain)
- [ ] Draft proposal within 24 hours
- [ ] Calendar follow-up for 3 days later
- [ ] Update CRM/spreadsheet with status

### Weekly Review

- [ ] Review all discovery calls from the week
- [ ] Calculate conversion rate (calls → proposals → closes)
- [ ] Identify patterns in successful calls
- [ ] Refine questions based on what worked
- [ ] Update call script with new insights
